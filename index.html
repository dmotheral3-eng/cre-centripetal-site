✓Ask · Answer

Ask the deal a question.
Get an answer, not a search result.

Questions in plain English; answers computed from the record itself — with the lines they came from, so you can check the checker.

"What's still owed to the Class B members before the promote turns on?"
"Which tenants are over their CAM cap this year?"
"How far is rental income running from what the memo projected?"
And when the record doesn't hold the answer, it says so. Ask never guesses, never fills a gap with a plausible number, and never dresses an estimate up as a fact. An honest "the record doesn't show that yet" beats a confident wrong answer every time.
